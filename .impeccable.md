## Design Context

### Users
Students looking for practical peer-to-peer help: tutoring, creative work, technology support, wellness, and campus services.

### Brand Personality
Energetic, trustworthy, sleek, and modern.

### Aesthetic Direction
A playful and student-friendly marketplace with Baylor University-inspired green and gold. Use warm cream surfaces, deep green primary actions, gold highlights, strong type hierarchy, and clear accessible contrast. Avoid corporate dashboards, generic purple gradients, and excessive decoration.

### Design Principles
- Keep browsing and posting simple for first-time student users.
- Pair bold campus energy with clear, dependable forms and navigation.
- Use WCAG AA contrast and visible keyboard focus states.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
